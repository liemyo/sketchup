# Amatu Sketch

SketchUp-style 3D modeling in the browser — live at **https://liemyo.github.io/sketchup/**

- Edge/face engine: closed coplanar edges become faces, lines split faces, Push/Pull with stretch/heal and cut-through, Follow Me, Offset, Intersect Faces, Solid Tools (union/subtract/intersect/trim/split/outer shell).
- Inference engine: endpoint, midpoint, on edge, on face, center, red/green/blue axis locks (arrow keys), parallel/perpendicular, from-point tracking, Shift lock, Measurements box (type values, `x5` / `/5` arrays).
- Default SketchUp shortcuts (Space, L, R, C, A, P, M, Q, S, F, T, B, E, O, H, Z, Shift+Z, G, K…), editable in Preferences › Shortcuts.
- Navigation: middle-drag orbit, Shift+middle pan, wheel zoom to cursor, trackpad two-finger orbit / Shift pan / pinch zoom, touch gestures. Preferences › Navigation sets the sensitivity of each (mouse orbit/pan/wheel zoom, trackpad swipe/pinch, touch orbit/pan/pinch), middle-button and two-finger actions, inversion, orbit pivot and mouse-vs-trackpad detection.
- **Image to 3D (AI)** (File › Image to 3D Model, toolbar, Components panel, or drop/paste a photo): the object is cut out on the device (plain background, or the IS-Net model shared with Amatu Hapus Background), sent straight from the browser to free image-to-3D models on Hugging Face Spaces (Hunyuan3D 2.1, TRELLIS, Stable Fast 3D, TripoSG, TRELLIS.2; Auto tries them in order within the visitor's daily ZeroGPU quota), then simplified (meshoptimizer), stood upright, coloured from the texture or projected from the photo (pose found by silhouette matching), reduced to a few materials and placed as a component at the real size you enter. Generated models are cached in the browser, so re-converting the same photo costs no quota. An optional Hugging Face token (Preferences › Image to 3D) raises the quota.
- **Render (Enscape / Lumion style)** (menubar **Render**, File › Render, toolbar): opens a live render window that puts the model on a generated site — **Rumah & taman** (plot with lawn, garden beds, driveway, footpath, fence + gate, hedges, a street with sidewalks, curbs, lane markings, street trees and lamps, neighbouring houses), **Jalan kota** (vehicles: road with lanes, shop houses, towers, scooters, traffic keeping left), **Taman** (plaza, paths, benches, flower beds, pond, scattered trees) or **Studio** (seamless backdrop) — chosen automatically from what the model is. Procedural entourage: trembesi/mango/ketapang/frangipani trees, coconut and areca palms, cemara, bougainvillea/ixora bushes, 3D grass, people (incl. hijab), cars (sedan, hatchback, MPV, SUV, van with Indonesian plates), buses, scooters; trees and cars never stand between the camera and the model. Physical sky (Preetham) with lit, self-shadowed clouds and cirrus, presets Cerah / Berawan / Pagi / Sore keemasan / Senja / Mendung, the sun at its real elevation for the hour and (by default) turned to light the facade you look at; sky image-based light, local reflection probe for glass, paint and water, soft shadows, ambient occlusion that ignores foliage, bloom, ACES/AgX tone mapping, grading (exposure, contrast, saturation, warmth, vignette, sharpen), two-point verticals. Library 2D people/trees/car blocks become 3D. **Render Foto** accumulates 24–160 jittered samples (anti-aliasing + soft sun) up to 4K and saves PNG/JPG. Navigate in the window (drag orbit, Shift/right pan, wheel/pinch zoom, WASD walk); settings are saved in the .amsk file.
- **Auto Paint** (Tools › Auto Paint, toolbar, or automatically inside Render without touching the model): recognises each object (building, vehicle, furniture, plant, person, site, other — from names and shape) and its parts, then assigns fitting materials: walls, plinth, roof tiles / flat roof, floors, terraces, ceilings, soffits, fascia, columns, stairs, window glass and doors (openings found from holes and recessed panels in walls), pools; vehicle paint with clear coat, tinted glass, tyres, alloy rims, head and tail lights, trim and livery stripes (big faces are first cut along the belt and window lines); wood/fabric/metal for furniture; bark, leaves and flowers; skin, hair, clothes. Styles Tropis modern / Minimalis / Klasik / Industrial. Only unpainted faces (or the grey placeholder colours of an Image to 3D mesh) unless “Warnai ulang semua”; one Undo reverts it.
- Groups & components with edit contexts, tags, materials (procedural textures), styles, scenes, shadows (sun position), fog, section planes with cuts & fills, Outliner, Entity Info.
- Export: working drawings (Gambar Kerja) to multi-sheet PDF and AutoCAD DXF with plans, elevations, sections, auto dimensions and title block; COLLADA `.dae` (opens in SketchUp via File › Import), OBJ, STL, glTF, 3D DXF, PNG/JPG, SVG.
- Import: DAE, OBJ, STL, GLB/GLTF, DXF (2D lines), images.

Native files are `.amsk` (JSON). Not affiliated with Trimble Inc.; “SketchUp” is a trademark of Trimble Inc.
